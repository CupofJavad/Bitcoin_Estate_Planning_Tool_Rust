# Logo Concept — Legacy Vault (GTM_PLAN §1.3)

**Product name:** Legacy Vault  
**Slogan:** Secure your crypto for those who come next.

---

## Design brief

- **Direction:** Trust, clarity, multi-chain, inheritance.
- **Symbol:** Stylized **vault door** or **key** combined with a **chain link** or **three small nodes** (BTC, XMR, STX) or a simple **inheritance** motif (one node branching to several). Current favicon uses vault-door + keyhole in accent `#0ea5e9`; can be evolved into the full symbol.
- **Style:** Clean, minimal; works at 16×16–32×32 (favicon) and at larger sizes. Single- or two-color (primary `#0f172a` + accent `#0ea5e9`) for light/dark backgrounds.
- **Wordmark:** “Legacy Vault” — sans-serif, clear hierarchy; match GTM typography (e.g. Inter, DM Sans, or system font stack).
- **Deliverables (reference):**
  - Primary logo: symbol + wordmark “Legacy Vault” (see [logo-concept.svg](brand/logo-concept.svg) for a sketch).
  - Symbol-only: already in use as favicon (`app/icon.svg`, `public/icon.svg`); can be replaced if this concept is adopted.
  - Favicon set: 16×16, 32×32, 180×180 (Apple Touch); optional 192×192, 512×512 for PWA.
  - Optional: dark-mode variant (e.g. light symbol on dark background).

**Placement:** Browser tab, app header, login, marketing pages, README, app-store assets.

---

## Current implementation

- **Favicon:** Vault-door shape with keyhole, stroke and fill in `#0ea5e9`. Files: `frontend/app/icon.svg`, `frontend/public/icon.svg`. No requirement to replace unless the full logo concept is adopted.
